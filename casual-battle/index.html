Bring the supplies home

Move your hero or a squad into the golden circle beside the wagon. Keep enemies out for 8 seconds to secure it.

Your squads fight automatically. Select one with its banner, a soldier or its card. Tap clear ground to move, or tap an enemy to focus it.

Follow stays near your hero. Hold defends a position. Attack advances toward the nearest enemy, then the wagon.
