# Battle sound recordings (`audio/sfx/`)

The battle soundscape (`js/core/battlesound.js`) plays these recordings. Every one is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is required; the authors are credited here with thanks. Each licence was checked on the sound's own page when it was downloaded (2026-10-18).

How the banks were made: freesound.org previews (the public MP3 previews: high quality `-hq` for voices, impacts and horns, `-lq` for the long beds and the crowd one-shots) were cut at MP3 frame boundaries, not re-encoded; the OpenGameArt WAV files were mixed to mono, resampled to 22.05 kHz and stored as 4-bit IMA ADPCM. The bank files (`battle-*.js`) hold them as base64; the game trims them, crossfades the loops and evens their loudness when it decodes them. Generated by a script; do not edit by hand.
