Skin silhouette cleanup (2026-11-13): face sampling no longer extends nearest skin colors into transparent source regions. Coverage remains inside the previous rig, with no extra painted skin or new detached pixels. Facial textures, feature anchors, recipes, clothing and long beard choices remain. Tests intentionally check face containment rather than equality with the older padded mask, plus connected skin and stubble containment across race/profile/width extremes.

Neckline review (2026-11-13): material sampling now starts at each source column’s visible collar edge, aligned with the combined clothing/armor neckline. This removes the flat vertical strips caused by sampling inside the authored neck opening. Bare torso follows its own fitted edge. Skin/jaw artwork and all coverage masks remain unchanged; source material proportions are retained. Collar coverage is checked across eight outfits, both profiles and extreme face/jaw widths, including open harnesses.

Skin detail integration (2026-11-13): age, scar and complexion layers now inherit local painted skin color and texture, replacing fixed gray/tinted strokes. Wrinkles use shallow crease shading and small reflected edges, with subtle under-eye volume. Freckles/sunspots use skin-relative pigment; healed scars/pockmarks use low-contrast relief. Pixel edges remain crisp; no image blur. Layer controls, all ages/mark choices and skin tint remain dynamic. Tests cover dark/light skin across all races, local contrast and eye/skin boundaries, distinct presets and clean young/unmarked faces.

Facial review (2026-11-13): painted eye and lip textures use a consistent feature envelope instead of per-column stretching, removing the wavy mouth distortion. Orc lip color follows the chosen skin with a smaller lip tint contribution. Small/heavy tusks keep their rig placement but render as mirrored pointed ivory canines with root shading and crisp edges. Nose bridges blend their cut upper edge into the underlying forehead while preserving nostrils. All dynamic layers and expression choices remain. Tests cover tusk taper/shading/root placement/symmetry at 256 and 512 pixels and nose-root color seams for every race.

Painted component redesign (2026-11-13): 50 authored face, hair and equipment components now supply actual RGB artwork through the existing fit masks, assembled as 19 editable transparent layers. Production game portraits use the new 256-pixel renderer. Composition, normalized version-7 recipes, current options, race proportions and seven tint channels remain. Continuous eye/mouth forms preserve every expression choice. Source atlases are bundled locally; no external generation service runs in game. The full offline player/NPC/collection workshop remains, with painted layer inspection/export, plus a focused 256-pixel art editor. Targeted checks cover every eye/mouth choice, fit masks across races/profiles/helmets, part independence, tinting, deterministic rendering, game integration and studio collection round trips.
