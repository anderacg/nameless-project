# Field-book management UI

Status: implemented on `codex`, 2026-10-23. This brings the approved cloth, brass and parchment mockup into the running game. All pictures use the existing pixel icons, equipment art, character portraits, main-menu landscape and white-tree emblem. UI type is Arial.

## Screens

- Character: the hero shares the troop-style Army inspector (unified 2026-10-24), with live Health/Stamina/Mana and expandable attributes, skill points, perks and equipment. Lords and Realms retain their information in the common frame.
- Army: Roster, Army skills, Condition and Passengers. Cards follow persistent battle order; all kinds share a detail layout. Rename, order, exact split, compatible merge and reinforcement keep their existing rules. Condition shows actual Fitness, Morale, Stamina, recovery, supplies, capacity, power and recruitment target. Recovering formations remain separate.
- Companions: the shared unit inspector, with Equipment, Character details, Company role and orders in expandable sections. Role/equipment choices retain Cancel/Apply. Occupied roles name their current holder; transfers retain exact source/destination, quantity, non-mutating Review and revalidation at Confirm. Company service includes dismissal.
- Inventory: equipment grid, six actual bag filters, selected-item comparison and pinned actions. All ten rings have separate targets. Original bag indices survive filtering. Two-handed displacement, overflow, bound quest items, letters and unavailable-equipment reasons remain visible; Details expands the inspection area on a narrow phone. Drag/drop and existing double-click equipment shortcuts remain available on desktop.
- Quests (a window of its own since 2026-10-23: Q, the Menu's Quests): book cover, coloured bookmarks, medallions, current objective, real progress, timing, reporting location and reward tiles. Main/Side/Jobs/Archive, tracking, full-map routing, stages, known locked offers and the archive use the current quest model. Longer facts remain in Details. Show on map, Track and Abandon sit in the quest's page under its objective (since 2026-10-23: the window's bar is its only bottom bar). Only jobs offer Abandon; its page states the actual penalty and Keep it is focused first. Back/Esc cancels that page.
- Skills: a focus around the selected skill, named Paths, full Fit and an accessible List containing every node. The graph adapts to its measured viewport; the selected skill and camera are independent for hero and each companion. Pan, wheel, pinch, cancellation and keyboard navigation do not buy skills. Only Learn spends a point. A pinned inspector states point, connection and refund requirements. Ten slot buttons open Cancel/Apply choice pages; automatic battle use and service validation remain unchanged. Disciplines and practice-based Basics have separate tabs.

## Layout and controls

The panel owns a fixed header, bounded content and the existing `nav()` bottom bar. Inner collections and details scroll independently, preserving context on refresh. The frame fits desktop, 844×390 landscape, 390×844 portrait and 320×568. A short landscape screen puts the title and primary navigation on one row. Portrait Army and Quests use list-to-detail navigation with Back. Inventory uses Bag/Equipment tabs and an optional expanded Details page. Companion portraits become a horizontal strip.

Choices use labelled buttons, tabs, cards and quantity steppers. No native dropdown appears in these management screens. Normal controls are at least 44 pixels high; equipment and ring targets are at least 44×44. Skill hit regions scale with the graph; Fit is for surveying the topology, with List available for precise selection. Numbers and words accompany colour. Tab rows support arrow/Home/End navigation. Reduced-motion preferences stop decorative motion.

## Ownership and implementation

`ui/fieldbook.js` owns the common frame, native-art cache, meters, rewards, scroll positions, steppers and draft choice pages (`fb-`). `ui/partypage.js` and `ui/companionpage.js` present existing party and companion commands (`party-`). `character.js`, `questwindow.js`, `inventory.js` and `talents.js` keep their action ownership and gameplay-service calls. Both HTML entry points load the new helpers in the same order. `fieldbook.css` is scoped to these windows and leaves world/battle presentation alone.

Selection, scroll, view and choice drafts are runtime UI state. No save-schema, quest-content, simulation, talent-cost, recruitment or equipment-rule change is part of this work (the Chronicle, below, added the save piece `chronicle`). The journal supports the Main category; it does not invent an unshipped main story.

## The menus, the Journal and the Chronicle (2026-10-23)

### The Game Menu (2026-10-23)

Status: built (agent GM), the owner's decision: one menu (`ui/hub.js`). Esc with no window open, Tab, and the bar's one Menu button (☰, the lower row; the old main-menu button merged into it) open it; Esc again or Resume goes back. The game is paused while it is open, in battle too. Inside a window Esc still steps back per the frame rule.
- **Header:** Paused · Day N · the world's name and kind · its seed, with Copy seed and World (the World page).
- **Tiles:** a tile per window from the `defineMenuEntry` registry (Army C, Inventory I, Quests Q, Journal J, Map M, Camp Z), each its icon and key. A tile opens its window, the menu closed first. Greyed with the reason: in battle every tile ("Not during a battle"); in the streets Army, Inventory, Map and Camp ("On the map only"), Quests and the Journal open; over a town "Leave the town first"; over any other window "Close the window in front first" (the bar's Menu can open the menu there).
- **System row:** Save game (greyed in battle, "Not during a battle", or mid-choice, "Finish what is in front of you first"), Load game (greyed with no save), Settings (the title's own page, `ui.js settingsPage`, Back to the menu and Resume; a change is stored as on the title), Main menu, Exit game.
- **Leaving:** Main menu and Exit ask first when play has moved on since the last save: "Unsaved progress since day N will be lost." (N: the day of this game's last save in any slot, made or loaded; "since you began" if never saved), with Save first (a quick save, then away), Leave anyway, and Cancel in the bar. Right after a save or a load, no question (`core/save.js unsavedSince`: the save record compared, its time and messages left out).
- **Phone:** on 844×390 the tiles in one row of six, the system row one row of five; upright (390×844) tiles three by two, the system buttons full width one under another. All on screen, 44 px controls.
- Its pages (save slots, Load, World, Settings, the question) step Back to the menu. The `esc-*` actions keep their behaviour; `esc-go` opens a tile, `esc-settings` Settings, `esc-leave` answers the question. Tests: `menu`, `journal`; screenshots: `tests/visual/gamemenu.js`.

Quests is its own window (Q), its tabs Main, Side, Jobs and Archive as before. The Character window kept Hero and Party until the Army window replaced it (below). The Journal (`ui/journal.js`: J on its last section, L on the Chronicle, the bar's Journal) holds sections as field-book tabs: Chronicle, Lords and Realms (`ui/lordsrealms.js`, moved from the Character window). `defineJournalSection(id, {label, icon, render, back, sub})` adds one (a Bestiary later). The last section is runtime state only. Quests and the Journal open over the map or the streets; Show on map is the map's only.

Lords and Realms discovery (2026-10-24): built. `world/knowledge.js` owns lasting discoveries and dated reports (`known`, optional save piece, empty defaults). Only known lords, realms, friends, holdings and relationship pairs appear; headers count known entries. Unknown lord names display as **???** through `lordName` (`world/knowledge.js`) on world tooltips and court labels, streets, hall cards, following labels, fight previews and unbought intelligence or war-work offers. Real names remain in model records, saves and quest matching. Direct audiences, confrontations and parleys identify their participants; heard news, bought information, chosen social dialogue and accepted named work identify only their subjects. Revealing a name updates the display without inventing remote whereabouts. The Journal still omits unknown lords. A meeting identifies its speaker; passive sightings and court visits update only lords already known. Opening an audience reveals no other names and consumes no rumours. Ask for rumours uses the existing news service when chosen; Ask about other lords reveals only the friends and rivals named in that answer, with no invented location or army information. Answers stay on screen through redraws without being heard twice. Other discoveries come from news and information actually heard. A lord’s location says **Last known**, with the event day and, for late news, the hearing day; reports keep their wording and army counts until a newer sighting, rumour, proclamation, arriving rider or purchased information replaces them. Distant movement and army changes do not update the Journal. Holding ownership is last known too, refreshed by seeing it or hearing its capture; global treasury and territory totals are omitted. News recorded but unheard, riders still travelling and intelligence offers reveal nothing. A new campaign resets discoveries. Tests: `lordnames`, `lorddialogue`, `journaldiscovery`, `journal`, `news`, `proclaim`, `worldlog`; screenshots: `visual/journaldiscovery`.

The Chronicle (`ui/chronicle.js`) replaced the Message log: every message, newest first under sticky day headers, on a dark ledger inside the brass frame. Its filters are tabs with counts (All, War & battles, Rumours, Your band, Story & quests; a message of no kind is under All only). Only one page (100 messages) is drawn, with Newest, Newer, Older and Oldest, so 20,000 open in about 10 ms. A quest's or story's message has Open in Main, Side, Jobs or Archive (the Quests window, its bar's Back returning to the Chronicle); a rumour's has Show on map while its "?" lasts (greyed, saying why, once faded). Controls are 44 px; on a narrow screen the filters take two rows and the pager's label its own. Tests: `tests/chronicle.js`, `tests/journal.js`; screenshots `tests/visual/chronshots.js`.

## The Army window (2026-10-23)

Status: built (agent AW), the owner's design. The Character window (Hero, Party) and the separate Skills window (K) became one window, Army (C, the Menu's Army, the bar's button), its header the company muster (`ui/partypage.js` lays it out; `character.js` keeps its commands). Its tabs: Roster, Army skills, Condition, Passengers. No gameplay rule changed.

- **The header** (on every tab, `partyHead`): the company muster, the standard, "Army", the band's name (shown once in the window), "N / 10 groups · fit + hero · wounded", and on its right the Fitness, Morale and Stamina bars with "On the road". It is to read, not a link (the owner): Condition is its own tab. On a short landscape screen (844×390) it is one row beside the tabs, the bars three % chips; on a phone the name, then the bars beneath. (`fbShow`'s `head` gives a window its own header.)
- **Roster** (the first): the Battle order, one list of cards, your hero, your companions and your troop groups in battle order (a companion leading its own party under "Away"), with New recruit group and Auto reinforce after the cards, in their scroll (on a phone too). Each card has its kind's pixel icon (a crown, a star, a shield: `rosterhero`, `rostercomp`, `rostertroop` in `ui/icons.js`) and an experience bar: the hero's and a companion's XP to the next level, a troop type's training ("N / M to train"; "Top tier" at the end of its path).
- **A card's detail** (unified 2026-10-24): every hero, companion and troop group uses the troop-style inspector: compact portrait, name and kind/level or tier; Manage group, Reinforce and Development in fixed positions; Group counts, Combat, Condition and Progress. Same width and responsive behavior for all three. Development opens the character's tree or the troop training path. Reinforce is disabled for individual characters; Manage is disabled for a companion leading another army. Inapplicable fields remain in place as dimmed dashes, with reasons; real zeros remain values. Health is per soldier for troops. Companion/troop campaign stamina and mana are unavailable because no individual persistent pool exists.
- **Development and details**: Development opens the hero's or companion's skill tree (K still opens the hero's), returning to the selected card, or the troop's training path (from/into, price, battle orders, traits). Four expandable sections follow Progress on every card: Character details (attributes, points, perks and a companion's disciplines/active skills; dimmed fields for troops), Equipment (hero inventory with Back to Army, companion Cancel/Apply choices, fixed troop loadout), Company role and orders (the companion's roles, reviewed army transfers and dismissal; unavailable for other kinds), and Unit details (traits, matchups, background). No companion-only top tabs or duplicated tree entry. Expansion state is runtime UI state per selected unit.
- **Army skills**: the band's skills (Command: Leadership, Tactics, Medicine, Engineering, Persuasion; the road: Scouting, Survival, Woodcraft, Hunting, Riding, Athletics; Trade and Stewardship), each its value, who gives it ("You 3 · Brann (Scout) +3 · Gear +1") and what it does. The band's skill is the hero's (`Leader.skill`): trained, plus a companion's role bonus (a Scout to Scouting, a Surgeon to Medicine: 2 and a third of theirs), gear and tree. The four roles are listed with their holder and effect (the Quartermaster's food, the Guide's march); Choose is a choice page (no one, or a companion; one holder each; Cancel/Apply).
- **Condition**: one scrolling area (recovery and reasons, supplies with Supplies beside them, capacity, power breakdown, recruitment target; the bars are the header's); a tab like the others (not a page of the Roster), its bar the same.
- **Passengers**: everyone who travels with you but does not fight, grouped: escorts, prisoners (Recruit, Release) and captives (Ransom, Recruit or Hand over, Let go). Prisoners lost their own tab, companions theirs.

Layout: on desktop the detail sits beside the cards; on a phone (≤650 wide) a card opens as a page with Back; on a short landscape screen (844×390) every kind stays beside the cards. Esc follows the bar (Manage, a page, then the Game Menu, then resume). Tests: `tests/army.js` (also: the muster in the header, once; each fixed page's last control in reach, its content at least 60% of the window's height, one bottom bar, at 844×390, 390×844 and 1280×800); the older suites (`fieldbook`, `characters`, `companions`, `talents`, `journal`, `escort`, `power`, `vitals`, `menu`, `attacks`, the army ones) read the new tabs. Screenshots: `tests/visual/fieldbook.js` (`scene=army|hero|companion|compskills|orders|troop|path|armyskills|condition|passengers`, `&w=&h=` the screen's size).
